## 3. Technical & Layout Architecture
- **Tech Stack:** React, Vite, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS.
- **Hosting & Deployment:** GitHub workflow deployed directly to Vercel production.
- **Style Rules:** High end presentation, definitive section dividers, structured information grids, mobile first ergonomics.
- **Strict Copy Rules:** Zero em dashes, zero en dashes, zero spaced hyphens, plain British English, zero AI emoji glyphs.

---

## 4. Jobcentre Plus & Universal Credit Context

### Goal: Proving Gainful Self Employment (GSE)
- **Work Coach Requirement:** Prove that the business is regular, organised, developed, and has a reasonable prospect of profit.
- **Objective:** Secure and maintain the 12 month start up period (exemption from the Minimum Income Floor).

### Key Evidence Assets:
1. **Live Platform:** Fully operational website showcasing live tools, interactive demos, transparent pricing, and clear contact routes.
2. **Weekly Commercial Logs:** Evidence of outbound prospecting, local trade directories audited, WhatsApp outreach sent, and quotes issued.
3. **Financial Records:** Cash in receipts, invoicing records, and allowable business expenses (hosting, domains, software, hardware, travel).
4. **Action Plans:** Documented 30 day, 60 day, and 90 day milestones for acquiring local trade clients.

---
